## 1. Business model

- Operator of the **Upper Tamakoshi Hydroelectric Project** in Dolakha district, Nepal — a **456 MW peaking run-of-river (PROR)** plant. **P** [ICRA Nepal rating report, 2022-11-23](https://www.icranepal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/A.127-Upper-Tamakoshi-Hydropower-Ltd..pdf)
- Revenue mechanism: single-asset generator selling 100% of energy to NEA under a long-dated Power Purchase Agreement (see §3). Earnings are therefore mechanically tied to (a) river flow / monsoon, (b) operational uptime, (c) the fixed PPA tariff. **P** (ICRA, ibid.)

## 2. Hydro type, capacity, units & status

- Type: **PROR (peaking run-of-river)** — generation depends on river flow; reservoir/peaking capability is limited; **no storage to smooth dry-season output.** **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Installed capacity: **456 MW**. **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Location: Dolakha district. **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Operational status (as of 2026-05-20, descriptive): plant operating; one major prior shutdown (88 days, Sep–Dec 2024 — see §7). **S-mirror** [myRepublica, 2024-12](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/upper-tamakoshi-resumes-producing-electricity-after-88-days-of-shutdown-676baf5f92413.html)

## 3. PPA terms / NEA exposure

- Off-taker: **Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA)**. Single buyer. **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Tariff: **wet season (mid-April → mid-December) ≈ NPR 3.63/kWh**; **dry season ≈ NPR 6.96/kWh**. ICRA further notes a **3% annual escalation for 9 years** applies on the base nominal rate. **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Vintage: PPA signed **more than 15 years ago**, **not revised**. Newer comparable projects price at materially higher tariffs (industry context). **P** (ICRA, ibid.) — for "industry context" framing: `[unverified — would need a NEA PPA comparator list]`.
- NEA payment-delay risk: a sector-wide concern in Nepali hydro; **not separately verified for UPPER's current receivables ageing** in this pass. `[unverified — needs latest UPPER audited receivables note]`.

## 4. Debt & finance cost

- **Debt-to-equity ≈ 88:12 (as of 2022 ICRA rating)**: equity NPR 10,590m vs **debt NPR 78,830m**. ICRA explicitly notes "high gearing levels (amid high debt and **negative net worth**)." **P** (ICRA, ibid.)
- Total project cost: **NPR 89,420m** vs original budget **NPR 35,294m** → ~**+153.4% cost overrun**. **P** (ICRA, ibid.) Corroborated **S-mirror** [ShareSansar, 2022-12-19](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/upper-tamakoshis-project-cost-increased-by-15455-operating-income-at-npr-7174-million-for-fy-2022-2022-12-19)
- **Interest During Construction (IDC) ≈ NPR 33,364m (~37% of total project cost).** **P** (ICRA, ibid.)

## 8. Operational, regulatory & event risks

### 8a. The September 2024 landslide / shutdown (verified)
- Date: ~**2024-09-27 / 28**; the landslide/flood destroyed the **sand settling pond (desander)**, **control room building**, and an **underground conduit**.
- Shutdown duration: **88 days**; power resumed **2024-12-24**.
- Fatalities: **four** (two company employees, two soldiers). **S-news** [Fiscal Nepal, 2024-09-29](https://www.fiscalnepal.com/2024/09/29/18139/landslide-buries-four-at-upper-tamakoshi-hydropower-project-status-still-unknown/) and **S-mirror** myRepublica (above).
- Insurance claim: **~Rs 1.78b** filed for income loss; **had not been received as of 2025-05-14** per myRepublica's reporting. Current resolution status post-May-2025: `[unverified — needs fresh update]`.

### 8b. Rolwaling Diversion Project (20.66 MW, growth/dry-season catalyst): construction halted
- ~**October 2025**: construction halted in the Gaurishankar Conservation Area because guidelines for land acquisition, EIA, and tree-felling in conservation zones had not been finalised. Underlying legal cause: a Supreme Court ruling that **struck down amendments to the National Parks Act** which had permitted infrastructure in protected areas. **S-news** [Kathmandu Post, 2025-10-17](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2025/10/17/delays-hit-construction-of-two-hydropower-projects-in-dolakha)
- Important nuance: this is properly described as a **Gaurishankar Conservation Area / Ministry of Forests procedural suspension rooted in the Supreme Court ruling**, **not** a Supreme Court order specifically targeting Rolwaling.
- Resumption status as of 2026-05-20: **no positive confirmation of resumption** found in this verification pass. `[partially confirmed — non-resumption is consistent with sources but not positively confirmed by a dedicated update]`.

### 8c. Structural/operational risk profile (descriptive aggregation of the above)
- **Single-asset concentration**: one PROR plant, one off-taker, one fixed PPA.
- **PPA price-rigidity vs cost base**: tariff fixed at legacy levels (§3) while project cost ended ~154% above budget (§4).
- **Disaster exposure**: 2024 landslide demonstrated material physical risk; insurance recovery slow.
- **Negative net worth (as of 2022 ICRA review)** + heavy gearing → balance sheet is sensitive to interest rates and to multi-quarter operational disruption.
- **Growth/dry-season offset (Rolwaling)** is procedurally stalled and unresumed in our sources.
- **Earnings are seasonal**: front-loaded into Q1 (wet-season tail), with losses in dry quarters typical (see §6).
