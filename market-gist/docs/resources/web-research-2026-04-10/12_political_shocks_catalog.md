## Complete Shock Catalog (2015-2026)

### Shock #1 — 2015 Gorkha Earthquake (April 25, 2015)
- **Event:** M7.8 earthquake, ~8,790 dead
- **NEPSE reaction:** Market closed for **19 working days**. Last pre-quake close 938.19. Reopened May 24/25, 2015, plunged **-28.37 points**.
- **Recovery:** Within ~1 month NEPSE was rising. Set record highs by July 2015 (1,881.45).
- **Driver of recovery:** NRB's Rs 8 bn paid-up capital mandate for commercial banks created M&A frenzy.
- **Pattern:** **One-off but template for major seismic events.** Academic studies (ACE/CIRD) found event-day t-stat significant and negative but **long-run impact NOT statistically significant** — market is resilient.
- **Trading rule (informal):** 19 trading days closed → buy the reopening dip in non-structurally-damaged BFIs.

### Shock #2 — India Blockade (Sept 23, 2015 – Feb 2016)
- **Event:** 4.5-month unofficial Indian blockade. ~2,000 factories shut, GDP growth 0.40%.
- **NEPSE reaction (paradoxical):** Despite economic collapse, NEPSE **kept climbing.** Hit new all-time high 1,881.45 on July 27, 2016 — immediately after the blockade ended.
- **Why:** Earthquake + blockade killed real estate and reduced loan demand → fixed-deposit rates fell → **liquidity flooded from real estate and banks INTO equities.**
- **Pattern:** **Durable & recurring.** The same dynamic repeated in COVID 2020-21.
- **Trading rule (informal):** "When real estate freezes and FD rates drop, long NEPSE."

### Shock #3 — COVID Lockdown (March 23 – June 12, 2020)
- **Event:** SEBON shut NEPSE March 23, 2020. Brief 2-day reopening May 11-12 hit circuit breakers. Full reopen June 15.
- **Sector damage:** **Hotels -19.97%, Non-Life Insurance -15.19%, Life Insurance -12.93%.**
- **Recovery:** Strong V-shape into massive bull run. Index from ~1,362 (mid-2020) to **3,198 peak August 18, 2021** — more than doubled in 12 months on excess liquidity.
- **Pattern:** Same liquidity-flight-to-equities dynamic as the blockade.

### Shock #4 — Oli Parliament Dissolution (Dec 20, 2020)
- **Event:** PM Oli dissolved House against his own party. Reinstated by SC Feb 22, 2021.
- **NEPSE reaction:** Short-term drop of ~90 points, circuit breakers triggered, then shrugged off. Closed **2,061.41 Dec 22**, rebounded to **2,126.90 Dec 23**, closed **2,068.45**.
- **Pattern:** **Important baseline.** Pure political crisis without economic policy change → short-lived reaction (<3 days). **Parliament dissolution alone is NOT a crash trigger.**

### Shock #5 — NRB Margin Lending Circular (July 2021)
- **Event:** Monetary policy 2021/22 capped margin loans at Rs 40m single-bank / Rs 120m multi-bank.
- **Reaction (delayed):** Index kept climbing to 3,198 peak August 18, 2021, then began sliding.
- **Follow-through:** **-44% drawdown** from 3,227 peak to 1,807 (June 2022). Margin debt had ballooned from Rs 40.9 bn (Mar 2019) to Rs 108.6 bn (Aug 2021).
- **Pattern:** **Margin-lending policy is the single biggest policy lever for NEPSE.** Recurring.

### Shock #6 — Sarbottam Cement / NEPSE CEO Insider Trading (Aug-Sept 2021)
- **Event:** NEPSE CEO Chandra Singh Saud and SEBON Chair Bhishma Raj Dhungana bought pre-IPO Sarbottam Cement at Rs 250 vs IPO price Rs 750 for relatives. Both resigned/suspended.
- **NEPSE reaction:** Combined with NRB margin tightening, produced **the largest single-day historical fall at the time: -108.26 points (-3.7%) on Sept 5, 2021**, with 5.03% intraday, 40-minute circuit break, ~31 companies down 10%.
- **Pattern:** **Regulator/NEPSE credibility shock + tight money = double-punch crash.** Recurring theme.

### Shock #7 — MCC Compact Ratification (Feb 2022)
- **Event:** Parliament ratified $500m MCC Compact on Feb 27, 2022.
- **NEPSE reaction (upside shock):** **+25.41 points on Feb 21** as ratification looked likely, led by hydropower. Specific names: United Idi-Mardi RB Hydropower, Samling Power, Terhathum Power, Barun Hydropower, Ghalemi Hydro, Nyadi Hydropower. But followed by **-43.12 points on Feb 28** — "sell the news" pattern.
- **Pattern:** **Hydropower sector is sensitive to US/China/India geopolitical news.** Recurring. Recently the US halting MCC funding has been a hydro-index headwind.

### Shock #8 — September 2025 Gen Z Protests ⭐ MISSING FROM OUR EVENT TABLES
- **Event:** Sept 8-21, 2025. Social-media ban triggered mass anti-corruption protests. 19+ dead. PM Oli resigned Sept 9, 2025. Sushila Karki became interim PM Sept 12. Parliament building burned.
- **NEPSE reaction:** **Market shut for ~2 weeks.** Before suspension, index fell 5 consecutive days to 2,672.25 on Sept 8. When it reopened **Sept 18, 2025, it crashed -160.33 points (-6.00%)** to 2,511.91 — **Rs 268 billion wiped in MINUTES.**
- **Trading lasted 3 minutes** before 4% circuit breaker triggered, 20-min halt, then triple negative circuit break forced full-day shutdown.
- **Sector:** Universal. **255 of 313 traded companies down.**
- **Recovery:** Interim govt → March 5, 2026 election → Balen Shah landslide → recovery rally into early 2026.
- **Pattern:** **Regime collapse = forced closure + multi-day reopening crash.** This is a replay of the 2015 earthquake closure template but politically driven. **This is the closest historical analog to any future political shock.**
- **THIS IS THE BIGGEST SHOCK IN OUR DATA WINDOW AND IT IS NOT IN OUR EVENT TABLES.**

### Shock #9 — March 2025 Pro-Monarchy Tinkune Violence (March 28, 2025)
- **Event:** Pro-monarchy rally led by Durga Prasai turned violent. Journalist + 1 civilian killed, 40+ buildings / 69 vehicles destroyed. Gyanendra Shah's passport seized. RPP leaders arrested April 2025.
- **NEPSE reaction:** Liquidity drain more than price shock. Specific index movement not surfaced. Became a leading indicator for the larger Gen Z collapse six months later.
- **Pattern:** Protest-driven liquidity drain.

### Shock #10 — Pre-Transition NEPSE Drop (March 28-30, 2026)
- **Event:** Market anticipation of new Balen Shah government / Wagle appointment. March 30: -47.71 points (-1.65%) close 2,831.39.
- **Pattern:** Pre-transition uncertainty drain.

### Shock #11 — April 1, 2026 Wagle Speech Crash #1
- **Date:** April 1, 2026
- **Minister:** **Dr. Swarnim Wagle** (newly appointed FM under Balen Shah PM, sworn in March 27)
- **NEPSE reaction:** **-74.73 points (-2.62%) close 2,776.36**. All 11 sectors red — total breadth failure.
- **Context:** Wagle's capital-market policy comment combined with arrest of businessman **Deepak Bhatta** (money-laundering probe, barred from leaving Nepal March 31) and fear over Integrated Digital Asset Registry proposal.

### Shock #12 — April 5, 2026 Wagle Speech Crash #2
- **NEPSE reaction:** **-105.50 points (-3.79%) close 2,676.68**. 261 of 268 stocks red. **7 companies hit negative circuits.** **Rs 179 bn market cap wiped in one day.**
- **Worst sectors:** **Reinsurance -8%, Hydropower -7.5%, Development Banks -7.4%**
- **Trigger:** Money-laundering arrests widened to **Sulabh Agrawal (Shanker Group vice-chair)**, one of Nepal's largest conglomerates (Rs 125 bn turnover, Rs 200 bn bank exposure, Rs 76 bn single-name exposure). Same day brought news that Rs 3.73 bn was allegedly diverted from 5 listed companies including **Himalayan Reinsurance** (explaining why reinsurance was worst sector). Five-businessman share-pooling cartel (Golchha, Ramesh Corp, Jawalakhel, Bhatta, Lucky Group) exposed.
- **Combined damage Apr 1 + Apr 5:** ~180 index points (-6.4% in two sessions).

### Shock #13 — April 6, 2026 Wagle Recovery
- **Event:** Wagle meeting with SBAN (Stock Brokers Association of Nepal). Said "investors should not panic, market is transparent," promised action against insider trading not small investors.
- **NEPSE reaction:** **+83.33 points** same-day rebound.
- **Pattern:** Minister-speech-down / minister-meeting-up is a **two-sided catalyst.**

### Shock #14 — April 8, 2026 Deuba Arrest Warrants
- **Event:** Arrest warrants for former PM Sher Bahadur Deuba + former FM Arzu Rana Deuba in money-laundering probe (part of broader investigation implicating 3 former PMs and 2 former ministers).
- **NEPSE reaction:** Marginal decline, turnover down — absorbed into the Apr 1-5 base.

## Recurring Patterns That Formalize Into Rules

### 1. Finance Minister speeches about capital markets are a dominant catalyst
ShareSansar's Mar 29, 2026 study **"The Impact of Finance Minister Appointments on NEPSE (2018-2025)"** catalogs 8 FM changes since 2018:
- One FM triggered **-12% in early weeks**
- Another an immediate **+200-point jump**
- Others a V-shaped recovery in 2 weeks
- Another **26-month highs**

**FM appointment is THE most reliable short-term catalyst on NEPSE.** This is its own potential signal.

### 2. Regulator credibility shocks compound with liquidity tightening
Sarbottam (Aug 2021) + NRB margin circular = -108 points, the largest historical crash at the time. Wagle speeches + money-laundering crackdown = -180 combined in April 2026. **Never a single cause — it's always policy + scandal simultaneously.**

### 3. Parliament dissolution alone is NOT a crash trigger
Dec 2020 dissolution: ~-90 points, shrugged off in 3 days. Political crisis without economic policy change = noise, not signal.

### 4. Full regime collapse IS a crash trigger
Sept 2025 Gen Z = -6% reopening crash, 2 weeks closed. 2015 Earthquake = 19 trading days closed. **Pattern: multi-day exchange closure → opening-day crash → recovery within a month.**

### 5. Liquidity reallocation is the dominant Nepal-specific mechanism
2015 blockade, COVID 2020. Any event that freezes real estate and depresses FD rates sends money INTO equities. **Counter-intuitive to Western frameworks.**

### 6. Hydropower sector is the geopolitical beta
MCC news, China/BRI news, US-Nepal relations, take-or-pay PPA disputes all show up in hydropower index first.

### 7. Bandhs (general strikes)
Mostly localized to Tarai-Madhesh in 2015-16; NEPSE closure during bandhs is administrative. **No surfaced academic study of "day-after-bandh" volume surge.** NRB study "The Economic Cost of General Strikes in Nepal" by Shrestha & Chaudhary (NRB Economic Review Vol 26 No 1) exists but doesn't cover NEPSE specifically.

### 8. Border disputes have NO measurable NEPSE reaction
Lipulekh/Kalapani May 2020, new Nepal map: no documented capital-market transmission.

### 9. Corporate corruption scandals matter ONLY when they touch listed companies
- **Bhutanese Refugee scam (2023):** No documented NEPSE impact — implicated politicians, not listed companies
- **Lalita Niwas (2020):** Implicated Goyal/Sarda/Chachan/Kedia/Bhatbhateni — none NEPSE-listed main entities. **No documented market reaction.**
- **Deepak Bhatta / Shanker Group (April 2026):** **Direct NEPSE crash** because Rs 3.73 bn was diverted from **5 listed companies including Himalayan Reinsurance.**

**The template:** "scandal hits listed-company cap table → crash."
