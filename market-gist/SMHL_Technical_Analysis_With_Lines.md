## 🎓 Learning from the Technical Lines

### **Why These Lines Matter:**

1. **Fibonacci Levels:**
   - Based on mathematical ratios found in nature
   - Traders worldwide watch these levels
   - Self-fulfilling prophecy: When many traders act on same levels, they become real

2. **Trendlines:**
   - Show the direction of momentum
   - Breaking a trendline = potential trend change
   - Respecting a trendline = trend continuation

3. **Support/Resistance:**
   - Previous price levels create "memory" in the market
   - Buyers and sellers remember these prices
   - They act as magnets for future price action
