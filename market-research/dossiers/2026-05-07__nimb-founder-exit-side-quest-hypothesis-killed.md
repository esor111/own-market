# NIMB Founder-Exit Side Quest — Hypothesis Killed; Buyer-Identity Question Opened

**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Trigger:** Side quest from W22 — investigate whether NIMB's six-founder exit (Feb 2026) is pre-regulatory derisking signal or noise
**Lineage:** Continues from dossier #5 (2026-05-05__fourth-verification-pass) which closed W19. This dossier addresses W22.

---

## Headline finding

The NIMB founder-exit signal **looks much weaker than the dramatic framing suggested**. Calibrated read:

- **~45% probability**: forced selling under operational/financial stress (dividend drought, ICRA downgrade horizon, profit slide)
- **~30% probability**: routine profit-taking via annual auction cadence (NIMB ran similar auctions Jan/Apr/Sep 2025)
- **~20% probability**: pre-regulatory derisking, NIMB is next leg of cleanup
- **~5% probability**: other (succession, family, etc)

The "NIMB is next leg of cleanup" hypothesis dropped from speculative ~50% to grounded ~20%. **Hypothesis killed cleanly.**

---

## Why the framing was misleading

| Frame | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Six founders simultaneously exit" | 488,309 shares = **0.143% of outstanding** — tail-end small founders, not control bloc |
| "Unprecedented event" | NIMB ran similar founder-share auctions Jan/Apr/Sep 2025 — annual cadence |
| "Pande exiting" | Wrong mechanism (registrar auction), wrong size, wrong price (52w low) — **almost certainly NOT Pande** |
| "Pre-Bhandari Commission move" | Filing predates ICRA downgrade by 6 weeks, predates Bhandari Commission by 2 months — sequence is consistent with insider knowledge of *operational* deterioration, not insider knowledge of *prosecution* |

## What I actually learned

### NIMB asset quality
- **NPL contradiction resolved.** 5.86% = gross NPA (ICRA Jan 2025); 1.53% = likely net NPA or stale Q1-Q2 print. Both legitimate, different metrics.
- **0+ DPD at ~37%** is the actually-scary number — one in three loans at least one day late. Leading indicator: NPA migration continues 2-3 quarters before stabilizing.
- **ICRA downgraded Mar 31, 2026 (A@ → A-)**, **removed Watch with Negative**. The downgrade *removed* the Watch — meaning ICRA thinks deterioration is now priced into the rating, not that further downgrades are imminent.
- **Net NPA / net worth ~9%** — solvency under stress is meaningful but not life-threatening.

### NIMB capital adequacy
- **CAR 12.95% (Feb 2025), 13.99% (April 2023)** — comfortably above 11% regulatory minimum.
- Two consecutive dividend skips (FY 79/80, 80/81) explained by **negative distributable profit** from merger goodwill / regulatory reserve from the NIBL+Mega merger eating into reserves. Mechanical, not punitive.

### NRB action
- **NRB issued formal warning to NIMB board and CEO** in Q2 of current FY for:
  - Disbursing loans exceeding guaranteed amounts without customer signatures
  - Interest Rate Spread gaps >2pp on similar loan categories
  - Quarter-end short-term loans violating Working Capital Loan Guidelines 2079 (**window-dressing the balance sheet**)
- **No fine, just warning.** Compare to NIC Asia, where former Chair and former CEO each got Rs 200K fines.

### Pande family CIAA history
- Dec 2018: CIAA filed graft case against ex-NRB Governor Tilak Bahadur Rawal, ex-Note Dept chief Upendra Keshari Paudyal, and **Himalaya Bahadur Pande** (Prithvi's brother, NPA Australia agent in Nepal).
- **July 2020: Special Court acquitted all three citing lack of evidence.**
- **No active reactivation.** Bhandari Commission's mandate covers political officials, bureaucrats, and family members — **not private bankers per se**. Path-to-relevance exists if mandate stretches to past NRB Governors, but no public sign that has happened.

## The genuinely interesting follow-up: buyer-side identity

Of the original 488,309 shares offered Feb 17:
- 488K shares offered → 253K reached public auction Mar 23 = **~48% absorbed by other existing promoters during the founder-only window**.

**This is control-tightening under stress, not derisking.** Someone inside NIMB's promoter group is consolidating. Without the NIBL Ace Capital registrar filing, we don't know who.

**If the net buyer is Pande or a Pande-aligned entity:** control-tightening operation under stress — a smaller, more local story than the cleanup-thesis but still meaningful. Reads as "the dynastic family is consolidating power as outsiders flee."

**If the net buyer is unrelated:** clean exit liquidity, no further read.

This is the question worth answering. The information is one specific lookup away — either (a) SEBON filing, (b) registrar filing at NIBL Ace Capital in Kamaladi, or (c) ShareSansar / Bajarko Chirfar follow-up reporting if they pick it up.

## Trade implication

**AVOID NIMB. Do not short.**

Reasons to avoid:
- Q3 result and ICRA downgrade now public — most operational bad news priced
- 0+ DPD 37% suggests 2 more quarters of NPA migration possible
- Cohort tag (Pande-Rana) is historically targeted *during regime changes* but rarely prosecuted
- NIMB's profile is "structurally impaired top-5 bank," not "next prosecution leg"

Reasons not to short:
- Top-5 bank with NRB implicit backing
- CAR 12.95% — capital adequacy fine
- Promoter consolidation = defensive moat
- NEPSE short-side liquidity is structurally weak

**Conditional opportunistic-buy trigger:** another 15-20% drop *without* CIAA action against Pande, AND Q4 82/83 shows DPD moderation. Until then, no position.

## Process learning

The dramatic framing ("six founders simultaneously exit") was a **frame trap**. The actual quantity (0.143% of outstanding) and mechanism (annual auction) reduced the signal materially. **Lesson:** when a finding triggers a research side-quest based on its dramatic framing, the first verification pass should be on the size/mechanism of the underlying event, before deeper interpretation. Flag for self-audit checklist.
