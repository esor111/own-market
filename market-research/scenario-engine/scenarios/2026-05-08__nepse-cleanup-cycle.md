## Scenario A — Vietnam Clock (Most likely)

**Probability: ~40%**

### Trigger chain
1. Second major arrest fires within 4-6 months of May 8 (Sandip Chachan, Raj Bahadur Shah, or Shubhi Agrawal) → W2 fires
2. Market reads it as "more to come" — same as Vietnam Oct 2022 (Truong My Lan arrested 6 months after Quyet)
3. NEPSE breaks below 2,620 support zone, tests 2,511 Sep-2025 trough
4. 2,511 holds (or breaks briefly before recovering) — capitulation flush fires
5. Market finds bottom in late-2026 to early-2027 window (10-16 months post Apr 5 arrest)
6. Trough zone: 2,200-2,511 (shallower than analog mean; domestic-only cleanup, no sovereign crisis)
7. Recovery toward 2,800 over 12-18 months post trough

### Discriminating signal
**W2** (Chachan/Shah/Shubhi arrest) fires within 6 months → Scenario A confirmed over C.
Watch also: NEPSE closes below 2,620 for 3 consecutive sessions → Scenario A over C.

### Portfolio response
- **On 2nd arrest news:** hold, do not buy. It's an acceleration signal, not a bottom signal.
- **On NEPSE 2,511 capitulation flush** (≥2.5% down + Rs 8B+ turnover in one session): deploy first 30% of cash incrementally over 10 sessions
- **Keep 50% cash for retest:** Scenario A says trough is single, not sequential — deploy 50% if 2,511 is retested and holds
- **20% cash for confirmation:** hold until A/D ≥ 1.0 for 2 of 3 sessions + turnover sustained above Rs 6B
- NABIL: hold at 18-20%; target Rs 595-610 (PRED-003)
- MBL: hold at 8%

### Timeline
- Second arrest: May–November 2026
- Capitulation flush: June–December 2026
- Trough: Q3–Q4 2026 or Q1 2027
- Recovery to 2,800+: 2027
