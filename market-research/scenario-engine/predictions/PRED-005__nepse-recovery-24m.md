# PRED-005 — NEPSE recovery to prior peak takes >24 months from Mar 24, 2026

## PREDICTED (2026-05-08)

### Question
Will NEPSE fail to close at or above 2,960 (the Mar 24, 2026 peak) before Mar 24, 2028?

### Resolution criteria
- **YES (outcome=1):** NEPSE does NOT close ≥ 2,960 at any point before Mar 24, 2028 (i.e., recovery takes >24 months)
- **NO (outcome=0):** NEPSE closes ≥ 2,960 before Mar 24, 2028 (i.e., recovery happens in <24 months)
- **Data source:** ShareSansar, HimalPress, NepseTrading — daily NEPSE index close
- **Ambiguity:** The 2,960 figure is the verified peak close (Kathmandu Post, Mar 24, 2026). Intraday highs don't count — use closing price only.

### Probability: 70% (YES = recovery takes longer than 24 months)

### Reasoning at prediction time

**Empirical base (from dossier #7):**
- 4 matched structural-cleanup analogs with broker/financial-sector default:
  - Vietnam 2022 (Blazing Furnace): VN-Index NOT recovered to prior peak as of 2025 (3+ years)
  - Bangladesh 2024: DSEX NOT recovered to prior peak as of 2026 (2+ years, ongoing)
  - Sri Lanka 2022 (Aragalaya): ASPI peaked mid-2021, still below peak as of 2026 (5 years)
  - NEPSE 2016-18 (margin crackdown): 28-month recovery
- 4 of 5 verified analogs took 24+ months to recover prior peak
- Only Egypt 2011 (Tahrir Square analog) recovered in <12 months — but that was a political revolution, not a financial-sector cleanup

**Current Nepal situation:**
- We are only 33 days in (May 8, 2026)
- Drawdown so far: −8.5% from peak
- Analog mean drawdown is −42%
- We haven't even reached the bottom yet → 24 months from March 2026 peak means Mar 2028
- Even Scenario A (most likely, shallowest cleanup) puts the trough in Q3-Q4 2026, then 12-18 months recovery → Mar 2028 or later

**Arguments FOR 70% (recovery takes >24 months):**
- 4 of 5 analogs support this
- We haven't formed a bottom yet (33 days in)
- Structural cleanups typically have multiple arrest waves (Vietnam pattern)
- Cooperative crisis (Rs 87.89B parliamentary probe) is still unresolved

**Arguments AGAINST (recovery in <24 months, 30%):**
- Scenario C (quick resolution, 12%): clean chairman + no second arrest + fast Bhrikuti resolution → NEPSE could recover in <12 months (like Egypt analog)
- NEPSE is a small, retail-dominated market — retail FOMO can drive fast recoveries
- If Bhandari Commission closes without expanding to banking, institutional confidence could return faster

**Note:** This prediction resolves March 24, 2028 — nearly 2 years away. It's the longest-horizon prediction in the system. Its value is in anchoring long-term patience (don't expect a V-shape).
