## How signals graduate to action

A watch signal becomes a trade trigger when:
1. The signal fires (event occurs)
2. The pre-stated trade response is checked against current state (price, liquidity, news)
3. A new dossier is written before any real-money action

No real-money trade should be taken purely on a watch signal firing without writing the dossier that documents the decision.
